LocalTask Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

LocalTask is provided by daccadelight.com.

Information we collect

LocalTask does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, location data, contacts, photos, or payment information. Tasks and settings are stored locally on your device.

Sharing and export

If you choose Export tasks, LocalTask sends the exported task file to the Android sharing menu. The app does not receive or control how the selected third-party app handles that information.

Analytics and advertising

LocalTask does not include advertising, analytics, or cloud synchronization.

Data deletion

You can delete individual tasks inside the app. Uninstalling LocalTask removes its locally stored data, subject to your device’s backup settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact daccadelight.com through its official website.
